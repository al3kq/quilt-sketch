About this sketch

A rough concept for a simple quilt layout planner, shown to ask quilters whether something like it would be worth building. Nothing is for sale and there is no account.

It does:

  • Square and half-square-triangle blocks you paint, turn and switch, with undo and redo
  • Your own fabric photos (cropped to a square, with a print size) and solid colours
  • Borders you can colour, several quilts you can duplicate to compare colourways
  • Cutting and yardage estimates for the top, borders, binding and backing
  • Saving to this device, a printable page, a picture, and a file copy

It does not (yet): sashing, curves, appliqué, paper piecing, block libraries, other programs' files, or syncing between devices.

The numbers are estimates. They assume ¼″ seams, plain strips and a usable fabric width you choose. Check them against your pattern before you cut.

There is no AI in it. The sample fabrics are simple patterns drawn in code. Your quilts and photos are saved only in this browser on this device; nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is tracked.
