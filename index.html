What is this?

A rough concept for a simple quilt layout planner, shown to ask quilters whether something like it would be worth building. Nothing is for sale and there is no account.

It does:

  • A grid of square and half-square-triangle blocks you can paint, turn and switch
  • Your own fabric photos, cropped to a square
  • Undo and redo, automatic saving on this device, and a printable page (Save as PDF)

It does not (yet):

  • Give yardage or cutting numbers: the picture is approximate
  • Borders, sashing, curves, appliqué or paper piecing
  • Open other programs' files, or sync between devices

There is no AI in it. The sample fabrics are simple patterns drawn in code. Your layout and photos are saved only in this browser on this device; nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is tracked.
